## What is the SAT?

A test many universities ask for, especially in the US. It is given **digitally** in the **Bluebook** app, takes **2 hours and 14 minutes**, and has two sections according to [College Board](https://satsuite.collegeboard.org/sat/whats-on-the-test/structure):

- **Reading and Writing**: 64 minutes, 54 questions.
- **Math**: 70 minutes, 44 questions.

## How is it scored?

Each section is from **200 to 800**, and the total score is from **400 to 1600** ([source](https://satsuite.collegeboard.org/scores/what-scores-mean)). Most weekend test results come out **2 to 4 weeks** after the test day ([source](https://satsuite.collegeboard.org/scores/score-release-dates)).

## Who asks for it?

- **The university itself:** each university decides whether it requires the SAT or makes it optional. On the page of each of the [Arruaad universities](/universities?track=arruaad) you will find its policy, from its official website.
- **KAUST (KGSP), last cycle:** 1350 with at least 700 in maths guarantees you an interview, and those who don't reach it may still have their application considered. [Source](https://kgsp.kaust.edu.sa/prospective-students)
- **The Custodian of the Two Holy Mosques Scholarship Program:** it doesn't require the SAT itself; your tests are whatever the university that admits you asks for. Details in [Tests: who asks for what?](/grades/grade-11#tests-title)

## What does it cost, and when?

- **Fees:** 68 dollars, plus an **international fee of 43 dollars** for those testing outside the US, and a test centre fee may be added at some locations ([source](https://satsuite.collegeboard.org/sat/registration/international-testing/fees)).
- **Upcoming dates:** 7 November 2026 (registration until 23 October), then 5 December 2026, then 6 March, 1 May and 5 June 2027, the same for international students ([source](https://satsuite.collegeboard.org/sat/dates-deadlines)). Look for a centre near you when you register.

## Sending your score to universities

- When you register for a weekend test, your score is sent to **4 places for free**, and you can choose or change them until **9 days** after the test; after that there is a fee ([source](https://satsuite.collegeboard.org/scores/sending-sat-scores/weekend)).
- **Score Choice:** you choose which test date's score you send, but some universities and programs ask for all your scores. This option doesn't include the free sends at registration ([source](https://satsuite.collegeboard.org/scores/sending-sat-scores/additional/score-choice)).

## How to start?

1. Take a **full practice test for free** in Bluebook ([official practice](https://satsuite.collegeboard.org/practice)).
2. Practise for free with [Khan Academy in partnership with College Board](https://satsuite.collegeboard.org/practice/khan-academy).
3. Find the score your universities ask for, and focus on your weaker section.
4. Take the test early (in Grade 11 or at the start of Grade 12) so you still have a date to retake it before applying.
