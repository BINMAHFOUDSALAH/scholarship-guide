## What are they?

They are run by the **National Center for Assessment (Qiyas)**, part of the Education and Training Evaluation Commission ([source](https://etec.gov.sa/ar/centers/qiy/p?id=4aea1743-5b9c-4833-bda8-5430b00dd2fa)):

- **The General Aptitude Test (Qudurat):** measures your ability to learn, not your knowledge of a particular subject: reading comprehension, seeing logical relationships, and solving maths problems built on basic concepts.
- **The Achievement Test (Tahsili) for science majors:** measures what you have learned in **biology, chemistry, physics and mathematics**.
- **The Achievement Test (Tahsili) for humanities majors:** for the Sharia track and the Business Administration track.

## Who asks for them?

- **Saudi universities:** each university sets the tests it asks for, “such as the Qudurat test and the Tahsili test”, and selects among applicants by the weighted or composite percentage ([Ministry of Education](https://www.moe.gov.sa/ar/knowledgecenter/eservices/pages/unified-admission.aspx), our translation).
- **The Custodian of the Two Holy Mosques Scholarship Program:** applying is possible **without a required** Qudurat or Tahsili score ([source](https://sites.moe.gov.sa/scholarship-program/conditions/)).
- **Aramco (previous cycle 2025–2026):** Qudurat 90% or more, and the new cycle's requirements haven't been announced yet ([source](https://www.aramco.com/ar/careers/for-saudi-applicants/student-opportunities/college-degree-program)).

So even if your goal is the scholarship, you may need Qudurat and Tahsili if you want Saudi universities to stay an option for you too. The full comparison is in [Tests: who asks for what?](/grades/grade-11#tests-title)

## Where to register?

On the **test registration platform** on the Education and Training Evaluation Commission's website ([e-services.etec.gov.sa](https://e-services.etec.gov.sa/)), which shows the announced dates and fees for each test.
