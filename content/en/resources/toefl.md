## What is TOEFL?

An English test from ETS accepted by most universities that teach in English, like [IELTS](/resources/ielts). It has four skills: reading, listening, writing and speaking, and it takes **about 2 hours** ([source](https://www.ets.org/toefl/test-takers/ibt/about/content.html)).

You take it at a **test centre** or at home (**Home Edition**) with live online supervision ([source](https://www.ets.org/toefl/test-takers/ibt/about/testing-options.html)).

## The new score scale {#scale}

Since **21 January 2026**, scores run from **1 to 6** in half points, for each skill and for the overall score, and the overall score is the average of the four skills ([source](https://www.ets.org/toefl/test-takers/ibt/scores/understand-scores.html)). Before that, the overall score was from **0 to 120**.

**Note:** many universities and programs still write their score on the old scale (such as 90). So for 2 years your score report shows **three scores**: the CEFR level, the 1 to 6 scores, and an equivalent score from 0 to 120 ([source](https://www.ets.org/toefl/institutions/ibt/score-scale-update.html)).

## Results and validity

- Your scores appear in your ETS account **3 days** after the test ([source](https://www.ets.org/toefl/test-takers/ibt/scores/get-scores.html)).
- The score is valid for **2 years** from the test date ([source](https://www.ets.org/toefl/test-takers/ibt/scores/understand-scores.html)).

## Who asks for it?

- **KAUST (KGSP), last cycle:** 90 in TOEFL iBT (or 7.0 in IELTS Academic) guarantees you an interview. [Source](https://kgsp.kaust.edu.sa/prospective-students)
- **The university itself:** each university sets the tests it accepts and their score. Look on the international admissions page of its website.

## Fees

They differ by country: choose Saudi Arabia on the [official fees page](https://www.ets.org/toefl/test-takers/ibt/register/fees.html) to see the price. The prices there don't include VAT.

## How to start?

1. Follow ETS's **free preparation plan**: eight weeks, with tips, activities and sample questions ([source](https://www.ets.org/toefl/test-takers/ibt/prepare.html)).
2. Make sure your universities accept TOEFL, and with what score.
3. Book your test well before the application deadlines, so you have time to retake it.
