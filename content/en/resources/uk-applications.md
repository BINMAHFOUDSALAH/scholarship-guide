## One application for five choices

You apply to all UK universities with one application on **UCAS**, with up to **five choices**: majors at one university or at different universities. The application fee for 2027 entry: **£34.50** for the five choices ([source](https://www.ucas.com/undergraduate/applying-university/filling-your-ucas-undergraduate-application)).

The application needs a **reference** from your school before you can send it ([source](https://www.ucas.com/applying/applying-to-university/dates-and-deadlines-for-uni-applications)).

## Dates for 2027 entry {#dates}

According to the [official dates page](https://www.ucas.com/applying/applying-to-university/dates-and-deadlines-for-uni-applications), with times in UK time:

- **12 May 2026:** UCAS registration opens for 2027 entry.
- **1 September 2026:** completed applications can be sent.
- **15 October 2026 (6 pm):** the guaranteed deadline for Oxford and Cambridge, and for most medicine, dentistry and veterinary medicine majors.
- **13 January 2027 (6 pm):** the guaranteed deadline for most majors.
- **30 June 2027:** anything arriving after it goes into **Clearing**, a stage for filling empty places.

Many universities accept applications from international students after these dates, so ask the university about available places first.

**Oxford and Cambridge:** you can't apply to both universities in the same year ([source](https://www.undergraduate.study.cam.ac.uk/apply/how)).

## The personal statement (Personal Statement) {#statement}

Its format has changed: instead of one text, it is now **three questions**, each answer **at least 350 characters**, and the total **4,000 characters** including spaces ([source](https://www.ucas.com/applying/applying-to-university/writing-your-personal-statement/how-to-write-your-personal-statement-for-2026-entry-onwards)):

1. Why do you want to study this major?
2. How have your studies and qualifications prepared you for it?
3. What have you done outside your studies that prepares you for it, and why is it useful?

UCAS's own advice: there is no “wrong” place for a piece of information between the second and third questions, the answers are read together so don't repeat yourself, and divide the characters according to your major and experience. How to write essays in general: [admission essays](/resources/application-essays).

**Artificial intelligence:** when you apply, you confirm that your statement wasn't copied from another source, including artificial intelligence programs, and UCAS checks for similarity between statements and may inform the universities ([source](https://www.ucas.com/undergraduate/applying-university/writing-your-personal-statement/guide-using-ai-and-chatgpt-your-personal-statement)).

## Is the Saudi secondary certificate enough? {#certificate}

It differs from one university to another, and many UK universities ask for a **foundation year** (Foundation) before the first year. The list at the bottom of the page brings together what each university's page says. The scholarship has extra conditions for funding the foundation year, which you will find on each university's page under the “Saudi secondary certificate” row.
