## What matters this year

1. **Your track is decided after this year.** Grade 10 is a common year, then each student is placed in a track according to their interests and abilities ([Ministry of Education](https://www.moe.gov.sa/ar/education/generaleducation/StudyPlans/Pages/SecondarySchoolTracks.aspx)). Moving between tracks is possible “according to a set procedure”, so ask your school about it.
2. **Your track may decide your options later.** Aramco, for example, accepted in its previous cycle only students from the general, computer science and engineering, and health and life tracks, and counted the Grade 10 average in its requirements ([source](https://www.aramco.com/ar/careers/for-saudi-applicants/student-opportunities/college-degree-program)). So your average starts now.
3. **English as a habit, not a test.** Our advice: 20 minutes a day on something you enjoy. You don't need IELTS or the SAT this year.
