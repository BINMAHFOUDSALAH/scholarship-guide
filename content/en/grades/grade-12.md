## What matters this year

1. **Universities abroad:** you apply to each university yourself through its platform (Common App, UCAS…), and the [US universities guide](/resources/us-applications) and the [UK universities guide](/resources/uk-applications) explain the platforms, fees and essays; some of their deadlines are in October and November. All the months are in the table below.
2. **Saudi public universities:** after the secondary certificate, through the [National Unified Admission Platform](https://www.uap.sa/). After the sorting results you must confirm your admission, because “not confirming cancels the student's right to the nomination” ([source](https://www.moe.gov.sa/ar/knowledgecenter/eservices/pages/unified-admission.aspx), our translation).
3. **The scholarship after admission:** in Arruaad and Emdad you first need an unconditional admission, and you can upload at most three admissions ([source](https://sites.moe.gov.sa/scholarship-program/conditions/)). Prepare its documents early: a valid passport, the secondary certificate, the admission letter, the academic record and your national address ([source](https://sites.moe.gov.sa/scholarship-program/submitting/)).
