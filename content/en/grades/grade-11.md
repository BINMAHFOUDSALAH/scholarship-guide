## What matters this year

1. **Know where you will apply before you prepare.** Saudi universities ask for what each university sets, such as Qudurat and Tahsili ([Ministry of Education](https://www.moe.gov.sa/ar/knowledgecenter/eservices/pages/unified-admission.aspx)). The Custodian of the Two Holy Mosques Scholarship Program doesn't require them ([source](https://sites.moe.gov.sa/scholarship-program/conditions/)). And universities abroad differ from one university to another. The table below brings this together.
2. **The summer before Grade 12 comes quickly.** Applying through the Common App opens on 1 August, before you start Grade 12 ([Harvard](https://college.harvard.edu/admissions/apply/first-year-applicants)). Our advice: take the test for the first time this year, so there is time to retake it.
3. **Your average still counts.** In its previous cycle, Aramco required 85% or more in mathematics, physics, chemistry and biology, from Grade 10 to the first semester of Grade 12 ([source](https://www.aramco.com/ar/careers/for-saudi-applicants/student-opportunities/college-degree-program)).
