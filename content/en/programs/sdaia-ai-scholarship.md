## How is it linked to the Custodian of the Two Holy Mosques Scholarship Program?

The SDAIA program **doesn't replace** the scholarship; it comes after it:

1. You get a **final, unconditional admission** from one of the target universities, in one of the program's majors.
2. You get a **scholarship decision** from the Custodian of the Two Holy Mosques Scholarship Program.
3. After that you apply to the SDAIA program, and go through the personal interview and the competitive selection for the available seats.

Of the 95 target universities, 82 are also in the [scholarship](/programs/custodian-scholarship) lists: 28 in the Arruaad track, and the rest in the Emdad lists. Search for them in [Universities in the scholarship lists](/universities) to see their rank and majors.

## When are applications?

Dates are announced on the [program's official page](https://sdaia.gov.sa/ar/Sectors/BuildingCapacity/Pages/ScholarshipProgram.aspx) and SDAIA's official accounts. Registration is through the [registration form on SDAIA's website](https://sdaia.gov.sa/ar/scholarship/Pages/form.aspx).
