## Conditional admission: when is it accepted? {#conditional}

The rule in the Arruaad, Emdad and Research & Development tracks is that your university admission must be **final and unconditional**. The conditions mention only one case of conditional admission, and the foundation year has its own conditions:

1. **The country's language:** if the country is **not English-speaking**, the admission can be **conditional on studying that country's language**, and the scholarship is available for studying the language (Arruaad, Emdad and Research & Development).
2. **The foundation year for the bachelor's (Foundation Year):** it isn't an exception to the rule, but studying it has extra conditions: **a high school average of 90% or more and IELTS 5.5 or more**, or its equivalent. The conditions don't name a particular country. [Source](https://sites.moe.gov.sa/scholarship-program/conditions/info-4/)

What does this mean in practice?

- An admission from a university in China, Japan or France, for example, whose only condition is that you study Chinese, Japanese or French first: **may** fall under the exception.
- An admission to a foundation year in the UK or the US, for example: you also need an average of 90% or more and IELTS 5.5 or more, and check with the Ministry that it is accepted in your track.
- An English-language year on its own in an English-speaking country: **the scholarship doesn't cover it**, and it isn't the same as a foundation year.
- An admission whose condition is a particular grade, finishing high school, or an English test: **the conditions don't mention it**, so don't assume it is accepted.
- A country being in the list doesn't mean all its universities are accepted. What matters is that the **university and the major** are in the list of **the same track** for the application year.
- Some countries have extra conditions: China (the average and IELTS for those whose admission is conditional on language) and Germany (level A2 in German). You will find them in the information at the top of the page.

### Before you rely on a conditional admission, ask yourself

1. Are the university and the major in the list of **the track you will apply to**, in **the official guide for the application year**?
2. What exactly is the condition written in the admission letter? Read it word by word.
3. Is the condition only **studying the country's language**, or a **foundation year** whose conditions you meet (90% and IELTS 5.5), or something else?
4. Is the country not English-speaking, and is the exception mentioned in your track's conditions: [Arruaad](https://sites.moe.gov.sa/scholarship-program/conditions/info-6/), [Emdad](https://sites.moe.gov.sa/scholarship-program/conditions/info-5/), [Research & Development](https://sites.moe.gov.sa/scholarship-program/conditions/info-7/)?
5. Is anything still unclear? Ask the Ministry directly through the [Tawasul service](https://tawasul.moe.gov.sa) or the number 19996, and keep the reply.

These questions help you understand, and don't give you a final ruling. The decision in your case belongs to the Ministry alone.

**Not yet decided by the official source:** does the country's language count, or the program's language of study? For example: a program taught in English in the Netherlands. If this is your case, ask the Ministry before you base your decision on it.

## University admission isn't the scholarship {#admission-vs-scholarship}

In Arruaad and Emdad you go through separate steps, and each step has a different body:

1. **Admission from the university:** you apply to the university directly, and the university issues the admission letter.
2. **The scholarship application:** you upload the admission with your documents on the application platform.
3. **Competitive selection and nomination:** you can upload at most three admissions, and only one application is chosen for you, based on the competitive selection and the available seats ([source](https://sites.moe.gov.sa/scholarship-program/conditions/)).
4. **After the nomination:** the e-services open for you, such as the financial guarantee, the scholarship decision and the embassy letter.

The program's website doesn't give a fixed application date ([FAQ](https://sites.moe.gov.sa/scholarship-program/faqs/)), so follow its announcements while you wait for university results. How to plan for it: [the application year, month by month](/grades/grade-12#year).

So you may get the university's admission and not be nominated for the scholarship, and an application other than the one you prefer may be nominated if you upload more than one admission.

## Questions students ask {#faq}

### When do applications close?

The program's website doesn't give a fixed date. Dates are announced on the program's website, its official accounts and the Ministry of Education's website. We put what we verify in “Latest updates”, with a link to the announcement.

### Do I need a visa when I apply?

No. You apply for the visa yourself later, and when your final admission to the program is confirmed, the embassy letter service becomes available to you.

### How many years does the scholarship cover for a bachelor's?

According to the university's system, as long as it isn't more than five years.

### What IELTS score do I need?

It depends on the university, and some cases have a minimum set by the program (such as the foundation year and China). We have brought them together on the [IELTS page](/resources/ielts).

### Is a companion or mahram required?

No, it isn't required.

### Is there a minimum high school average?

There is no specific average, according to the [program's FAQ](https://sites.moe.gov.sa/scholarship-program/faqs/). What matters is that you meet the general conditions and the track's conditions, and that you have an unconditional admission. The exception: anyone admitted to a foundation year needs an average of at least 90% ([extra conditions](https://sites.moe.gov.sa/scholarship-program/conditions/info-4/)).

### Can I study online or part-time?

No. Arruaad and Emdad are for in-person study ([Arruaad](https://sites.moe.gov.sa/scholarship-program/paths/path-arruaad/), [Emdad](https://sites.moe.gov.sa/scholarship-program/paths/path-emdad/)), and one of the general conditions is full-time study while living in the scholarship country ([conditions](https://sites.moe.gov.sa/scholarship-program/conditions/)).

### How many university admissions do I upload in my application?

Three admissions at most, and one application among them is chosen based on the competitive selection results and the available seats ([conditions and application policies](https://sites.moe.gov.sa/scholarship-program/conditions/)).
