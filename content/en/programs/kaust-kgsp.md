## How does the program work? {#how}

KAUST **doesn't admit you to the university**. You apply to the universities yourself, the program assesses your application, and if it chooses you, it gives you the scholarship offer. Details of its benefits are given only to those who get the offer. The steps in the last cycle, according to the [Prospective Students page](https://kgsp.kaust.edu.sa/prospective-students):

1. Apply to strong universities, preferably among the [Arruaad track universities](/universities?track=arruaad).
2. Take the SAT and an English test (TOEFL or IELTS).
3. Send the program's online application (free) with your scores and essays, and update it with your admission results.
4. An interview with the admissions team and one of the program's graduates ([KGSP news, 20 May 2026](https://kgsp.kaust.edu.sa/news/news/2026/05/20/coming-full-circle-kgsp-alumni-interview-new-applicants-for-cohort-18)).
5. An offer from the program, then an orientation meeting at KAUST before travelling.

## If you are in Grade 10 or 11 {#earlier}

Don't apply now, but prepare: choose a major in science, technology, engineering or mathematics, and learn about the [SAT](/resources/sat), [IELTS](/resources/ielts), [TOEFL](/resources/toefl) and [which tests each program asks for](/grades/grade-11#tests-title). Many of the program's students took part before it in KAUST programs for high school students such as STEPs and SRSI ([KAUST news](https://www.kaust.edu.sa/news/kaust-prepares-70-gifted-student-program-scholars-for-study-abroad)).
