# KAUST Gifted Student Program (KGSP): fact-check, 2026–2027 (research by Claude)

**Checked:** 2026-10-11, by Claude, with WebFetch / WebSearch on official KAUST pages. Nothing here is reviewed by the owner yet.
**Scope:** برنامج كاوست للطلبة الموهوبين (KGSP) only. Sources used: `kgsp.kaust.edu.sa`, `www.kaust.edu.sa`, `academy.kaust.edu.sa`, `leap.kaust.edu.sa` (older KAUST page). Third-party sites were used only as hints (section 8).
**Not accessed / limits:**
- WebFetch returns text processed by a reader model, not the raw page. Quotes below are as extracted; the owner should spot-check the key ones in a browser.
- The application portal (`100943engagecms.campusnexus.cloud/kaust-portal/...`) only shows a "create account" form. I did not register, so I could not see the form itself.
- The **offer benefits** (what is paid for): KGSP says they are private and shared only with students who get an offer.
- KAUST/KGSP accounts on X, Instagram, etc. were not read.
- News archive pages 3–10 (older than Jan 2025) were not read.
- No page about KGSP was found on `moe.gov.sa` or `sites.moe.gov.sa`.
- KGSP pages have **no publication dates** in the body (footer says "2018 © KGSP" everywhere). Dates below come from the article URL or from the dates stated in the text.

## 4. How selection works (Cohort 18 cycle)

From [prospective-students](https://kgsp.kaust.edu.sa/prospective-students) unless noted. ✅ for Cohort 18.

1. **Register and apply online** (link «HERE» → KAUST portal). Disclose personal info, essays, and «your latest technical test scores». **No application fee.**
2. **Update** scores and university results after submitting, before the deadline.
3. **Interviews** (mid-February → early April 2026) with the admissions team and a KGSP alumnus. Spring 2026: «16 alumni participated in panel interviews with 155 student applicants» ([news, URL 2026-05-20](https://kgsp.kaust.edu.sa/news/news/2026/05/20/coming-full-circle-kgsp-alumni-interview-new-applicants-for-cohort-18)). Not interviewed by early April = not advancing.
4. Interviewers look for a strong technical background, wanting a world-class undergraduate education, wanting to contribute to the Kingdom, and understanding KAUST.
5. **Offers** by 23 April 2026; a waiting list is possible.
6. **Onboarding:** pre-departure orientation at KAUST (23–25 June 2026), arrival abroad by mid-August ([KAUST news](https://www.kaust.edu.sa/news/kaust-prepares-70-gifted-student-program-scholars-for-study-abroad); [Cohort 18 news, URL 2026-08-04](https://kgsp.kaust.edu.sa/news/news/2026/08/04/welcoming-kgsp-cohort-18)). 70 students joined.

Arabic summary: تقدّم إلكترونيًا (بلا رسوم) ← حدّث درجاتك ونتائج قبولك ← مقابلة ← عرض من البرنامج ← لقاء تعريفي في كاوست ← السفر.

## 9. What a grade 10–11 student can take from this (official facts only)

- You apply in **grade 12**; before that, prepare. (§3 #1)
- Last cycle's score guides: SAT 1350 (Math 700), TOEFL 90 / IELTS 7.0. (§3 #2–3)
- Apply yourself to strong universities, ideally on the **Arruaad top-30 list**. (§3 #4; the Arruaad list is in [custodian-scholarship-2026-27.md](custodian-scholarship-2026-27.md))
- Choose a STEM major. (§2 #1, #4)
- KAUST Academy runs high school programs (STEPs, SRSI, HSSP…) that many KGSP students joined first; not required. Links: [KAUST Academy](https://academy.kaust.edu.sa).
