# UX audit and targeted redesign (2026-10-16, decision 061)

**How it was done:** the live site walked at 375px (homepage, grade 11, explorer with the UK filter, Manchester) and the code and data read. **Not done:** no students talked to, no analytics, no real-phone or screen-reader test. **[V]** = verified on the site or in data · **[H]** = a hypothesis for the usability test.

## The five most important problems (impact × confidence)
1. **Requirements gaps [V].** 57 European and 12 English-speaking universities showed «لم نجمعها بعد». Oxford and UCL were hidden drafts. Duolingo was unknown in 41 of 121 published files.
2. **Duolingo invisible [V].** No guide; `/search?q=duolingo` returned nothing; result rows and «باختصار» showed IELTS only.
3. **Text about the site itself, and repeated disclaimers [V]:**
   - «كل معلومة رسمية مع رابط مصدرها» and «كل نتيجة معها مصدرها»
   - the program page's independence line
   - the admission ≠ scholarship notice, three times
   - «SAT/ACT: لم نتحقق منه» on UK pages, where SAT doesn't apply
4. **Homepage program rows long on phones [V observed, impact H].** Four links per program.
5. **No UK process guide or essay guidance [V]**, though 30 UK universities have data and essays are a required step for most US universities.

## Kept (it works)
- The grade-first hero and its constellation.
- «مفتوح للتقديم الآن» with «لمن».
- The explorer's list rows (already compact on phones; not a table, so no card conversion).
- The university page order.
- Minimum vs recommended (058).
- The grade-page structure (038).
- The sky design system (060). It is three days old and approved; its real-phone check is still open.

## Direction
Keep the sky as the one visual language and refine, don't restyle. Each reference's role:
- **GOV.UK / UCAS lead:** task-first pages, plain words, one answer per row.
- **Linear:** discipline (one row pattern, one tag style, shared macros).
- **Apple / Stripe:** restraint only (less text, fewer links).

Rejected:
- a new pathway motif (it would compete with the constellation, which already shows the three-year progression)
- more editorial storytelling beyond الرواد
- any new framework or dependency

## Deliberately not done
- An «تقبل Duolingo» explorer filter. It would hide unresearched universities and suggest "not accepted"; the Duolingo page's computed list answers the question. Revisit after the usability test.
- Palette or typography changes.
- Result rows as cards.

## Questions to add to the usability test [H]
- "Can you use Duolingo instead of IELTS for Manchester? For Purdue?" (finds the row, reads «ليس ضمن الاختبارات التي تذكرها الجامعة» correctly)
- "How many essays does Yale ask for?"
- "Does the Saudi certificate get you into Oxford's first year?"
- On the homepage: do students miss the removed links (the X account, «جامعاته وتخصصاته»)?
