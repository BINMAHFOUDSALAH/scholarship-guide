# Spec: Universities explorer

**Status:** approved by the owner 2026-10-11, with these choices: include the doctorate (البحث والتطوير) lists; Arabic + English names; an advanced explorer, not a simple form; per-university requirements researched by agents.

## Goal
Help a student answer, fast:
1. Is university X on the official list for الرواد, إمداد, or البحث والتطوير, at what rank, in which field?
2. I want major Y: which universities can I go to, per track, and at what rank?
3. What does university X ask for (English scores, SAT, deadlines, acceptance rate)?
4. Is it in a non-English-speaking country, where the language-study exception might apply?

The explorer never says "you are eligible". It shows what the official list says, and links to the PDF page and the university's own pages.

## Source: the official guide PDF
`الدليل الاسترشادي لترتيب قوائم الجامعات حسب المجالات للعام الأكاديمي 2026–2027` (75 pages, `data/raw/`, git-ignored, re-downloadable).

| Pages | Content |
|---|---|
| 5 | **الرواد**: one ranked list of 30 (all fields: «مسار الرواد يدعم جميع التخصصات», program site) |
| 7–13 | البحث والتطوير (doctorate): fields and their majors |
| 14–42 | البحث والتطوير: a ranked list per field (5 fields, 145–161 each) |
| 44–50 | **إمداد**: fields and their majors (some majors tagged «درجة الماجستير فقط») |
| 51–74 | **إمداد**: a ranked list per field (8 fields, 63–69 each) |

Each list entry: rank, English name, country flag, and sometimes a note: «درجة الماجستير فقط» (master's only), «للأشخاص ذوي الإعاقة (الإعاقة السمعية)», or «درجة البكالوريوس للتخصصات التالية فقط» + a list of majors.

**Known issues in the PDF** (recorded, never silently fixed): the R&D ICT list jumps from 90 to 100; p74 hides «Utrecht University 70» under «University of Lausanne 66» (we follow what's visible); Groningen shows a German flag on p36 and Erasmus a Swiss flag on p38 (Dutch elsewhere); Tilburg University shows the French flag (we show the Netherlands, with a note); "Visual Storytelling & Art Directio" is cut off in the PDF.

## Data
### `data/universities.json` (generated by `scripts/extract_universities.py`, never edited by hand)
- `source`: title, URL, academic year, retrieval date.
- `fields`: per track (`rnd`, `emdad`): `id`, `name_ar`, `name_en`, `majors` [{`name_ar`, `name_en`, `masters_only`}], `majors_pages`, `list_pages`.
- `universities`: `id`, `name_en`, `country`, `listings` [{`track`, `field` (null for الرواد), `rank`, `pdf_page`, optional `masters_only`, `hearing_impaired_only`, `bachelors_only_majors`, `label_ar`, `box_ar`}], `flag_mismatches`, optional `country_note`.
- Helper files: `scripts/flag_countries.json` (flag hash → country), `scripts/university_aliases.json` (same university, different spelling), `scripts/list_overrides.json` (hidden cells, rank gaps, country corrections).

### `data/university_names_ar.json`
`{id: Arabic name}`. **Our translation** (the guide has English names only). Always shown with the official English name next to it.

### `data/university_details/<id>.json` (one per university, researched from official sources)
```json
{
  "id": "harvard-university",
  "name_en": "Harvard University",
  "checked": "2026-10-11",
  "entry_ar": "القبول لخريف 2027",
  "admissions_url": "https://college.harvard.edu/admissions",
  "platform": {"value_ar": "Common App أو Coalition", "source_url": "https://..."},
  "english": {
    "ielts": {"min": 7.0, "note_ar": "...", "source_url": "https://..."},
    "toefl_ibt": {"min": 100, "note_ar": null, "source_url": "https://..."},
    "duolingo": null,
    "note_ar": "لا تشترط الجامعة اختبارًا محددًا..."
  },
  "sat_act": {"policy": "required", "note_ar": "...", "source_url": "https://..."},
  "dates": [
    {"event_ar": "آخر موعد للتقديم", "round_ar": "Restrictive Early Action", "cycle_ar": "خريف 2027",
     "date": "2026-11-01", "year_stated": false, "source_url": "https://..."}
  ],
  "acceptance_rate": {"percent": 3.6, "scope_ar": "القبول العام لدفعة خريف 2025 (Common Data Set 2025–2026)", "international_percent": null, "source_url": "https://..."},
  "language_note_ar": null,
  "not_found_ar": ["نسبة قبول الطلاب الدوليين غير منشورة رسميًا"]
}
```
Rules:
- Every value has a `source_url` on the university's own site or its official application service (UCAS, Common App, OUAC…). No rankings sites, no forums, no consultants.
- A number appears only if the official page states it. "No minimum" is recorded as `null` + a note. Anything not found goes in `not_found_ar`, never guessed.
- `sat_act.policy` ∈ `required`, `optional`, `not_considered`, `not_applicable`, `unknown`.
- Dates are structured (decision 038), never free text:
  - `event_ar`: what happens. Use «آخر موعد للتقديم» for application deadlines; the grade 12 page and «أقرب موعد» look for it.
  - `round_ar`: the round, optional. English names stay English (`Early Decision / Early Action`), so they don't mix directions.
  - `cycle_ar`: the intake, e.g. «خريف 2027».
  - `year_stated`: false when the official page gives only the day and month, so we inferred the year. The site then shows «السنة غير مذكورة في الصفحة».
  - Optional `note_ar` (time zone, who it applies to) and `reference_only: true` for an older cycle shown for reference. Reference dates never count as the next deadline.
- English tests (decision 058), each of `english.ielts` / `toefl_ibt` / `duolingo`:
  - `min`: the score the page states as the minimum for full admission, or `null`. A recommended, competitive, expected or "typical" score is **never** a `min`.
  - `recommended` (optional): that recommended / competitive / expected score; `recommended_source_url` when a different official page states it (e.g. a state-system minimum plus the campus's competitive score).
  - `each_min` (optional, IELTS): a uniform minimum in every skill; uneven per-skill minimums stay in `note_ar`.
  - `varies: true` (optional): the level depends on the program; `min` is the general or lowest one.
  - `accepted: false` (optional): the test doesn't count as proof of English there (an explicit statement, or an exhaustive list without it), shown as «لا يُقبل لإثبات اللغة». No score then. A university may still read it as extra material (Georgia Tech).
  - TOEFL: a value ≤ 6 is on the new 1–6 scale (since 21 January 2026), larger values on 0–120; the page says which.
- `admission_tests` (optional): `{note_ar, source_url}` for admission tests some programmes require (ESAT, TMUA, UCAT, LNAT…).
- `application_fee.waiver_source_url` (optional): when the waiver is on a different page from the amount.
- `draft: true` (optional): research not yet verified on the official page (a 403, a snippet). Hidden on the site; shown with `WADIH_SHOW_DRAFTS=1`.
- Never record a number from a search snippet or a third-party site. `language_note_ar` is only for the language of teaching.
- `saudi_certificate` (optional, decision 054; mainly UK): `{"direct_entry": true | false | null, "note_ar", "quote", "source_url"}`. Whether the Saudi secondary certificate alone gets a student into year 1, from the university's own Saudi Arabia / country page, with the exact English `quote`. Shown as «الشهادة الثانوية السعودية» only when `direct_entry` is true or false; `null` means the page doesn't say (and the gap goes in `not_found_ar`).
- Non-English-speaking countries: `language_note_ar` says what the language of instruction is and whether English-taught undergraduate programs exist (with source).

## Pages
- **`/universities`**: the explorer.
  - Search by university name (Arabic or English) **or by major** (Arabic or English).
  - Filters: track (الرواد / إمداد / البحث والتطوير), field, country, "English-speaking country or not".
  - Choosing a major shows: every الرواد university (all majors), then the إمداد universities ranked in that major's field, then (if chosen) البحث والتطوير. Each row: rank, name (Arabic + English), country, notes («ماجستير فقط»…).
  - Instant filtering in the browser (a small self-hosted script, no libraries); without JavaScript the full list still shows and the search falls back to a normal form.
- **`/universities/<id>`**: one university.
  - الرواد: rank + «يمكنك دراسة أي تخصص» (all majors, with the official source).
  - إمداد / البحث والتطوير: every field it's ranked in, its rank, the PDF page, and the majors in that field (with master's-only tags and notes).
  - Requirements (if researched): English scores, SAT/ACT policy, dates, acceptance rate, each with its source.
  - Non-English-speaking country: a note linking to the conditional-admission section.
- **`/api/universities`**: the data as JSON.
- **Since decision 035:**
  - Applied filters are shown as removable tags + «مسح الكل», drawn by the server.
  - Choosing a university in the suggestions opens its page.
  - Result links carry `from=<search>`, and the university page links back to the same results (only known parameters and values).
  - The rank is explained as the position in the list, not a world ranking.
  - The university's own next deadline is labelled as such.
  - A university page starts with «باختصار» and folds the doctorate lists.
  - «قائمتي» saves universities on the device (`/my-list`).
- Header: an «الجامعات» link.
